NextUp Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

NextUp has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your calendar stays on your devices. The only things that reach us are the feedback you choose to send and your questions to Ask NextUp.

This policy covers the NextUp apps for iPhone and Mac, their widgets and Live Activities, and the NextUp Garmin watch face. "We" means Luca Orth, the developer of NextUp.

Your calendar

You connect NextUp by pasting the address of a calendar feed. Your iPhone or Mac downloads the feed straight from your calendar provider, and the events are stored only on your devices. We never receive your calendar.

  • The feed address and any access token are stored in your device's settings and Keychain.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, they sync between your iPhone and Mac through your iCloud account, along with your NextUp settings. Apple handles that sync under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Demo mode uses made-up meetings and doesn't connect to anything.

Location and travel time

If you turn on Travel time, NextUp uses your location to ask Apple Maps how long it will take to drive to your in-person meetings, so it can tell you when to leave. Your location and the meeting address go to Apple to calculate the drive time. They are not sent to us or stored by NextUp. You can turn Travel time off in Settings, or turn off location access in iOS Settings, at any time.

Garmin watch

If you connect a Garmin watch, NextUp sends your upcoming meetings (titles, times, places, and meeting links), your calendar feed address, and your watch face style to the watch through the Garmin Connect app over Bluetooth. The watch face may also refresh the feed on its own through Garmin Connect. This data stays between your phone, the Garmin Connect app, and your watch; Garmin's handling of it is covered by the Garmin Privacy Policy.

Alerts and notifications

Alarms, notifications, widgets, and Live Activities are created on your device from your calendar. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Feedback and Ask NextUp

These are the only features that send information to us, and only when you use them.

  • Feedback. When you send feedback, we receive what you wrote, the screen you sent it from, the category and urgency you picked, the app version and build, and your device type and OS version (for example, "iPhone · iOS 26.0").
  • Ask NextUp. Your questions and the answers are saved with the same app and screen details, so we can answer them and improve the user guide. Answers are written by an AI model from OpenAI, which receives the recent messages in that conversation. Under OpenAI's API terms, it doesn't use that data to train its models.
  • To keep your reports and chats together without an account, NextUp creates a random, anonymous ID on your device. It isn't tied to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other identifier.
  • This data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider.

Please don't put sensitive personal information in feedback or chats. We use this data only to support NextUp and make it better. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or combine it with data from anywhere else.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your devices is deleted when you delete NextUp. We keep feedback and Ask NextUp conversations for as long as they help us support and improve the app. To have yours deleted, email us and tell us roughly when you sent it. We'll remove it.

Children

NextUp isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change affects what leaves your device, we'll mention it in the app's release notes.
